How long is each lane?

Learn lane corridors from moving vehicles. Review the four corners on the frozen image: the first edge is START, the opposite edge is END. Enter the actual road distance between those edges in metres. Use a straight section for curved roads.

Incoming video keeps training the path suggestions during setup. Draft suggestions replace lane shapes in this editor; saved lanes change only when you apply.

Live approximate km/h uses perspective-corrected motion within a straight, planar lane and your entered distance. Review corners carefully: incorrect distances or boundaries affect speed accuracy. Completed gate crossings also provide average km/h. Interrupted tracks are excluded.

Setup freezes only your displayed frame. Click to draw; drag existing vertices to adjust. Lines: negative → positive counts as IN when role is IN; the reverse counts as OUT. Arrow directions use image coordinates.
